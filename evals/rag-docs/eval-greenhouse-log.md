# Greenhouse log, spring season

This log is sample data for the retrieval suite of the local-agent-stack evaluations; the
varieties and the greenhouse are fictional.

## Sowing and germination

| Variety | Sown | Germinated after | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ember Cherry tomato | 2 March | 6 days | Strongest seedlings of the season |
| Blue Arrow pea | 9 March | 11 days | Sown in deep trays |
| Lemon basil | 12 March | 9 days | Needs 22 °C at night |

## Watering

Each tray gets 1.5 litres of water per day, split between morning and evening. The rain barrel
holds 400 litres and lasts about two weeks in April.
